On iOS 27: removing Adrift's parts

Removing one app in Adrift?

Keep the parts. Adrift's two parts, Adrift Opens and Adrift Closes, serve every app Adrift tracks. After you remove an app in Adrift, the parts keep listening, and Adrift forgets that app's opens and closes the moment they arrive. If you also built parts for that app yourself (say, “TikTok Start” and “TikTok Stop”), those keep counting until you delete them in Shortcuts.

Leaving Adrift altogether?

The parts come out as easily as they went in. Parts first, then the app.

Step 1 of 3

In Shortcuts, open All Shortcuts. Touch and hold Adrift Opens, tap Delete, then Delete Shortcut. Do the same for Adrift Closes, and for any copy ending in “2”.

iOS asks once more, and means it: the delete reaches every iPhone on your Apple ID. When you're leaving, that's what you want.

This shortcut will be deleted from all of your iCloud devices. Delete Shortcut

Step 2 of 3

Delete any parts you built yourself the same way: the ones named after your apps, or “App”, “App 2” if you built them before iOS 27.

Step 3 of 3

Delete the Adrift app itself, like any other app.

Parts first, then the app.

That's the whole exit. No account to close, nothing left running, nothing left behind.

On iOS 26 or earlier: the 20-second cleanup

Leaving is fine — the tracker was always yours, not ours. On iOS 26 or earlier it's two tiny automations per tracked app in Apple's Shortcuts app, and they come out as easily as they went in. On iOS 27, see removing Adrift's parts above.

About 20 seconds · two swipes per tracked app

Deleted the app before the automations? Until they're removed, iOS shows an “Automation failed” banner over the tracked app on every open and every close. Inside the broken automation, Shortcuts reports:

Unknown — this action could not be found
Update Shortcuts

That “Update Shortcuts” link is a dead end — updating Shortcuts fixes nothing. Deleting the automation does. Steps 1 and 2 above are still the whole fix.

Coming back?

Reinstalling Adrift revives your automations automatically — no re-setup, they just start working again. If one direction still errors after a reinstall (opening records, closing fails), open that automation in Shortcuts and back out without changing anything. Viewing it is enough to re-link the action.

Questions

What happens when I lock my iPhone?

The timer stops when you lock. When you unlock back into the app, Adrift can't tell, so leave the app and open it again, and the timer starts again. It's the same on every iOS version. Adrift never counts time it can't see: it would rather undercount than guess.

Do I need two parts for every app?

On iOS 27, no. Adrift's two ready-made parts cover every app Adrift can track, and Adrift counts only the apps you've added.

On iOS 26 or earlier, yes. A part's “When” block can watch several apps at once, but its timer step can record only one, so every session from the other apps would be logged under the wrong name. One Start part and one Stop part per tracked app is what keeps your numbers honest.

Which iPhones show the island timer?

Ones with a Dynamic Island: iPhone 14 Pro and 14 Pro Max, and every iPhone since — the 15, 16, and 17 lines, and iPhone Air — except the iPhone 16e. There, the timer counts at the top of the screen while you scroll. On every other iPhone, the timer is a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in Notification Center instead of the island. Reminders are the same everywhere — banners at the thresholds you chose, arriving mid-scroll, island or not.
