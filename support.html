Leaving Adrift? The 20-second cleanup.

Leaving is fine — the tracker was always yours, not ours. It's two tiny automations per tracked app in Apple's Shortcuts app, and they come out as easily as they went in.

About 20 seconds · two swipes per tracked app

Step 1 of 3

In Shortcuts, open the Automation tab.

Not the Shortcuts tab — Automation, in the middle.

ShortcutsAutomationGallery

Step 2 of 3

Swipe left on each Adrift automation to delete it. Every tracked app has two — one for opening, one for closing. Both go.

Swiping all the way left deletes in one motion — no Delete tap needed.

When “Instagram” is closed · Stop App Timer Delete

Step 3 of 3

Delete the Adrift app itself, like any other app.

Automations first, then the app — in that order you'll never see an error.

That's the whole exit. No account to close, nothing left running, nothing left behind.

Deleted the app before the automations? Until they're removed, iOS shows an “Automation failed” banner over the tracked app on every open and every close. Inside the broken automation, Shortcuts reports:

Unknown — this action could not be found
Update Shortcuts

That “Update Shortcuts” link is a dead end — updating Shortcuts fixes nothing. Deleting the automation does. Steps 1 and 2 above are still the whole fix.

Coming back?

Reinstalling Adrift revives your automations automatically — no re-setup, they just start working again. If one direction still errors after a reinstall (opening records, closing fails), open that automation in Shortcuts and back out without changing anything. Viewing it is enough to re-link the action.

Questions

Can one automation cover all my apps?

No. The Shortcuts trigger can watch several apps at once, but the action inside can record only one, so every session from the other apps would be logged under the wrong name. One open and one close automation per tracked app is what keeps your numbers honest.

Which iPhones show the island timer?

Ones with a Dynamic Island: iPhone 14 Pro and 14 Pro Max, and every iPhone since — the 15, 16, and 17 lines, and iPhone Air — except the iPhone 16e. There, the timer counts at the top of the screen while you scroll. On every other iPhone, your timer lives on the Lock Screen: lock, glance, it's counting. Reminders are the same everywhere — banners at the thresholds you chose, arriving mid-scroll, island or not.
