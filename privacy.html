Privacy

Last updated October 4, 2026

Adrift is built local-first: your sessions, notes, and settings never leave your phone. This page explains the one exception, anonymous analytics, in plain language.

Your sessions stay on your phone

Every session Adrift times — which app, when, for how long — is stored only on your device. There is no account, nothing to sign into, no server holding your sessions, and no way for us to read them. Your notes to yourself are yours alone; they never leave the phone.

The one exception: anonymous analytics

Adrift sends anonymous analytics — counts, not content — through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. Adrift's own events tell us things like "someone finished setup" so we can see where the app loses people. They carry no session contents, no note text, and nothing that says who you are: no name, email, or Apple ID. The sessions Adrift times are sent only as coarse buckets (like "3–6 minutes"), never exact lengths, though session_ended, like every event, carries the date and time, which is usually the moment the session ended. Each of Adrift's own events also carries this install's random group letter (a/b/c/d), kept only to compare app variants.

Adrift 1.0.1 and earlier also send three events that 1.0.2 retired:

  • demo_session_started
  • funnel_beat_completed (which step, answered or skipped)
  • plan_summary_viewed

TelemetryDeck, the library that sends these events, also sends a signal when it detects a new install, and each time Adrift starts (even in the background, such as when a timer starts or stops) or comes back after five minutes away. It adds standard details to every event: a scrambled ID for this install, a random one for each visit, your iPhone model, iOS and app versions, language, region and time zone, display and accessibility settings such as text size, the date and time, how often and how long Adrift runs, and the day you first opened it. The scrambled ID is a one-way hash made on your phone, never your name or Apple ID. No screen contents, and no app usage beyond what's here.

Purchases

Adrift Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card, your name, or your Apple ID. StoreKit tells the app only the state of your purchase or trial (whether it's active, when it ends, and whether it renews) and whether a free trial is still open to your Apple ID.

What we don't do

No tracking across apps or sites. No advertising identifiers. No selling or sharing of data — there is nothing to sell. No third-party SDKs beyond TelemetryDeck. The parts you set up for Adrift live in Apple's Shortcuts app, on your iPhone, under your control: you can see them or remove them there anytime. If Shortcuts syncs them to your other devices through iCloud, that sync is Apple's; Adrift keeps no copy of them and can't read them. On iOS 27, the two ready-made parts listen for every app Adrift can track that's on your iPhone when you add them; Adrift counts only the apps you've added and forgets the rest the moment they arrive. If Adrift isn't running when a part reports an open or close, iOS starts it in the background, so TelemetryDeck's start signal (above) goes out with that moment's date and time, even for an app you haven't added. The signal never names the app.
